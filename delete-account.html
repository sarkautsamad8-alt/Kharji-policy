Delete your خەرجی (Paying Friends) account

You can request account and associated personal-data deletion without installing or signing in to the app.

For immediate in-app deletion, open the account menu → سڕینەوەی هەژمار, verify the same account through Google's sign-in page, then confirm permanent deletion. The app never asks for your Gmail password and does not delete your Google account. If you cannot sign in or delete the account, use the support email below.

  1. Email sarkautsamad8@gmail.com from the Google email address used for the app, with the subject “خەرجی account deletion”.
  2. State that you want your app account and associated personal data deleted. Do not send a password, one-time code or access token.
  3. Support will verify account ownership, explain the handling of shared records and any retention period, process the request, and confirm the outcome by email.

Email a deletion request

If that button does not open an email app, send the request yourself to the address above. Clicking it opens a draft; it does not send email or immediately delete your account.

What deletion covers

Deletion removes your Supabase Auth account, sign-in sessions and personal profile. It can proceed with unpaid debts: shared amounts and records remain, with your profile name removed and account identifier replaced by a different random identifier in each room. Deletion does not forgive a debt. Pending repayments involving the deleted account are voided without changing confirmed balances; room administrators can record later off-app settlements, explicitly marked as administrator-recorded. A remaining active member becomes admin if needed. Rooms with no other active members are deleted. Email support requests are handled manually after ownership verification. Signing out, uninstalling the app or leaving a room alone does not delete your account.

Shared financial records remain while the room exists; the app does not automatically delete them after a fixed period. If account deletion leaves no active members in a room, that room and its history are deleted. User-written expense descriptions remain in shared records and may contain identifying details, so removing the profile name and replacing the account identifier does not guarantee anonymity.

The app has the same request option under the account menu → سڕینەوەی هەژمار.
